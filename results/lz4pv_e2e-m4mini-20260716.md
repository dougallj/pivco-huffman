# lz4pv: LZ4HC parse + pivcoh v4 entropy, end-to-end

`extras/bench/bench_lz4pv.c`: the four-stream LZ4 split (lz4hc_split)
with literals + tokens + offset-high-bytes entropy-coded by pivcoh v4
(off-low + overflow raw; per-stream raw fallback), executed by the new
plane-aware split decoder (`lz4_split_decompress_planes` — offsets as
two independently codable byte planes, stride-specialized core, old
interleaved API preserved).  Lens wire built with
-DPIVCOH_LENS_WIRE_NO_MODE2 (decode-priority).  `PIVCOH_EFFORT` env
selects simple/balanced/faster/fastest; `LZ4_LEVEL` the parse (default
HC-9, matching the May analysis).  lz4 v1.10.0 clone at ext/lz4
(untracked) supplies lz4hc.h/lz4.c + the reference decoder.

## Positioning (same mini, same corpus)

* zstd-3 ratio at LZ4 speed: lz4pv balanced = **3.187 ratio at
  6.1 GB/s decode** vs zstd-3 3.200 at 1.79 GB/s (vanilla) / 3.191 at
  2.01 GB/s (our pivcoh-zstd) — ~3x the decode of even the improved
  zstd at equal ratio, and decode parity-to-faster vs plain LZ4 while
  compressing 17-24% denser.
* The May pivco is beaten easily: its literals-stream decode was
  4510 MB/s on cat-wiki (old scalar pivcoh); v4 balanced runs the
  same stream at ~8-15 GB/s (2-3x), silesia lits geomean 11.6 GB/s.
* Effort tiers barely move e2e decode (lits entropy is a modest share
  once execution dominates) but shaped trees lift the lits stream
  ~25% over simple; balanced is the sensible default.
* Per-file: text-heavy files decode FASTER than plain LZ4 (dickens
  +30%, reymont +13%) — the plane-split execution beats LZ4's
  interleaved parse and the input is smaller.  Barely-compressible
  files (sao -20%, x-ray -14%, mozilla -9%) pay entropy passes for
  little ratio; a per-stream "skip coding when gain < threshold"
  knob would recover most of that.

Raw column meanings in the bench: enc = parse+entropy e2e; dec = full
container decode (entropy + execution); litMB/s = literals stream
entropy decode alone (the May-comparable number).
